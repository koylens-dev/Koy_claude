# Staff guide (print this and stick it near the counter)

Sign in at **/staff/login** with **your own** email and password. Never use someone else's login: every action is recorded with your name.

## Attendant: Orders screen

1. **Tap "Start shift"** every time you open or reload the page. It switches on the loud alarm and keeps the screen awake.
2. **New order (alarm ringing, red card):** check the items and the address.
   - **Accept** → choose how long it will take (15–60 min). The customer gets an SMS with the time.
   - **Reject** → choose a reason. The customer is **refunded automatically**. Only reject if you really can't make it.
   - The alarm rings until every new order is accepted or rejected. "Mute 1 min" if you're on the phone.
3. **Mark ready** when the food is packed (if the kitchen didn't already).
4. **Delivery:** tap **Send out** → type the rider's name and phone → tap **Send address to rider on WhatsApp** → **Mark out for delivery**. The customer gets the rider's number and a delivery code.
5. **Pickup:** tap **Ready for pickup — notify**. The customer gets an SMS. When they collect, tap **Collected**.
6. When the rider confirms, tap **Delivered**.
7. **Phone or WhatsApp customers:** use **New order**. Add the dishes, the customer's name and number, and the zone/landmark, then **Create & get pay link**. The customer gets the link by SMS; you can also tap *Send via WhatsApp*. **We never take cash.** The kitchen sees the order only after it's paid.
8. **Sold out?** Open **Sold out** and switch the dish or extra off. Customers see it immediately. Switch it back on when it's available.
9. Phone icon = call the customer. **WA** = WhatsApp the customer. Printer icon = print the ticket.

If the red **"Offline — reconnecting"** badge shows, check the Wi-Fi or switch to mobile data. Payments are still safe; the screen catches up by itself.

## Kitchen: Kitchen screen

- **Tap "Start shift"** (sound + screen on).
- New tickets beep and appear in **To cook**. Tap **Start** when you begin, then **Mark ready** when it's done.
- The timer turns **amber** near the promised time and **red** when it's late. Tell the attendant if you'll be late; they can call the customer.
- Yellow boxes are the customer's notes: read them every time (allergies, "no onions").
- Scheduled orders (blue) appear 90 minutes before you need to start them.

## Managers

- **Refunds:** on any paid order → *Partial refund…* (amount + reason) or *Cancel & refund*. All refunds go into the audit log.
- **Admin → Sales** for the day's numbers. **Admin → Orders → Needs attention** for anything that failed.
- **Pause orders** (power cut, gas finished, overloaded): Admin → Hours & settings → *Accepting orders* off. Add a short message for customers.
- New staff, or someone leaving: Admin → Staff.
