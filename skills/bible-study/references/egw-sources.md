# Ellen G. White sources

## Rules

1. Quote verbatim only when you are certain of the wording, book and page.
2. Otherwise paraphrase and cite the book and chapter title ("In *The Desire of Ages*, chapter 'In Pilate's Judgment Hall', she describes…").
3. If web search is available and you are unsure, search egwwritings.org and use the exact text found there.
4. Never blend her words with yours inside quotation marks. Use "…" when you shorten a quote.

## Where to look, by kind of passage

- **Gospels and the life of Jesus:** *The Desire of Ages* (the chapter covering that event).
- **Sermon on the Mount (Matthew 5–7, Luke 6):** *Thoughts from the Mount of Blessing*, with one chapter per section of the sermon. The two builders are in its final chapter.
- **Parables:** *Christ's Object Lessons*.
- **Acts and Paul's letters:** *The Acts of the Apostles* (for example "Apostasy in Galatia" for Galatians).
- **Genesis to the reign of David:** *Patriarchs and Prophets* (for example "Idolatry at Sinai", "The Rejection of Saul").
- **Solomon to the return from exile, Daniel and the prophets:** *Prophets and Kings* (for example "Captives in Babylon", "The Fiery Furnace").
- **Revelation, prophecy, the end:** *The Great Controversy*.
- **Prayer, faith, conversion, growing in Christ:** *Steps to Christ*.
- **Character, education, integrity, work:** *Education*.
- **Health, service, family, compassion:** *The Ministry of Healing*.
- **Home and marriage:** *The Adventist Home*.

## Quotes with well-established wording

Use these freely where they fit the theme:

- "The greatest want of the world is the want of men—men who will not be bought or sold, men who in their inmost souls are true and honest, men who do not fear to call sin by its right name, men whose conscience is as true to duty as the needle to the pole, men who will stand for the right though the heavens fall." (*Education*, p. 57) — integrity, courage, people-pleasing.
- "Character building is the most important work ever entrusted to human beings…" (*Education*, p. 225) — foundations, obedience, growth.
- "Prayer is the opening of the heart to God as to a friend." (*Steps to Christ*, p. 93) — prayer, relationship with God.
- "Keep your wants, your joys, your sorrows, your cares, and your fears before God." (*Steps to Christ*, p. 100) — trust, anxiety, casting cares.
- "Christ's method alone will give true success in reaching the people." (*The Ministry of Healing*, p. 143) — witness, service, evangelism.

For anything else, paraphrase or verify first.
